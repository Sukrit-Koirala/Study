## 13. Limitations
- No genuinely untouched third data split exists -- Experiment B's 'individually tuned alpha' is EXPLORATORY, not an independent generalization result.
- Statistical reliability uses an i.i.d. (not sequence/block-aware) bootstrap over query positions.
- GPT-2 confidence groups use predetermined quartile bins, not development-tuned thresholds.
- CRISP's S6 diagnostic (CRISP + exact masses) is not part of R6's required M-list and was not computed.
- The tuned-config reproduction check (Phase 1) covers raw/DIME only, not a tuned-CRISP evaluation -- CRISP is only ever fit/evaluated at the matched rank=8 configuration, consistent with R5's precedent.
