## 1. Research hypotheses
H1-H6 as specified in the module docstring and research mission.

## 2. Exact mathematical definitions
F~_j(q)(y) = p_j(y)*exp(delta_{j,y}(q)) / sum_z p_j(z)*exp(delta_{j,z}(q)); delta_{j,y}(q) = sum_l theta_{j,y,l}*phi_l(q); fit by ridge regression onto z_{q,y}=log(F_j(q)(y)/p_j(y)). See module docstring for the full baseline definitions B0-B6.

## 9. Limitations and potential confounders
- 66 of 3811 clusters evaluated (Phase 1-3 scope); storage figures are per-cluster MEASURED marginal bytes for the evaluated sample, not extrapolated to all 3,811 unless stated.
- Basis family fixed to linear_anchor_diff throughout -- basis-family comparison was R1's scope, not R4's.
- E8 (adaptive rank allocation) not implemented -- see Observation 5 / H6 for the motivating signal.
- E6 bounded to the top-10 highest-probability labels per cluster, not an unbiased sample of all label pairs.
- The centering ablation (Part 7) covers only 20 of the sampled clusters at a single rank/lambda, a deliberately bounded ablation, not a full sweep.
- No LLM interpolation or perplexity anywhere in this script, per the spec's explicit instruction -- all comparisons are distributional against the exact within-cluster evidence oracle.
