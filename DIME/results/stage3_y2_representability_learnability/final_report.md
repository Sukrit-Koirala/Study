## Limitations
- Focused pilot: 5 clusters (per the spec's own 'approximately 3-5' instruction), not a large-scale study.
- No document/sequence-grouped split exists in the cached data.
- No additional held-out or training-source text is available beyond the three existing cache files -- natural query scarcity is a real, reported limitation, not resolved by this delivery.
- Boundary-enriched points are heuristically PLACED (nearest label-differing member-pair interpolation) but their TRAINING TARGETS are always the exact teacher, never hand-labeled or approximated.
- MLP capacity grid limited to widths [128, 512] x depths [2, 4], not the full aspirational grid, per the spec's own 'initial configurations, not mandatory Cartesian product' instruction.
- The historical Y1 validation population has been used repeatedly for research decisions across this project; this delivery's own real-data comparisons remain exploratory, per the spec's own instruction.
